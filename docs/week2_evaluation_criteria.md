# Evaluation Criteria — UniGuide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Project Title:** UniGuide: An AI-Powered Academic Information Assistant for UTM Undergraduate Students

---

## 1. Introduction

This document defines the evaluation criteria for UniGuide, an AI-powered academic information assistant designed to help UTM undergraduate students access academic information through conversational interactions.

The evaluation focuses on determining whether the system can provide accurate academic answers, retrieve relevant information from official UTM documents, generate understandable responses and handle questions outside its defined knowledge domain.

The evaluation criteria are linked to the project objectives and requirements defined in `week2_requirements.md`.

## 3. Evaluation Capability Categories

The evaluation criteria are grouped into four capability categories:

### 3.1 Domain-Specific Capability

Evaluates whether UniGuide understands UTM academic information and retrieves relevant content from official university documents.

Relevant criteria:
- Domain-specific Answer Accuracy.
- Source Retrieval and Citation Relevance.

### 3.2 Generation Capability

Evaluates the quality and clarity of the answers generated by the system.

Relevant criterion:
- Response Clarity and Understandability.

### 3.3 Instruction-Following Capability

Evaluates whether UniGuide follows its defined scope and handles unsupported or unrelated questions appropriately.

Relevant criterion:
- Out-of-Scope Query Handling.

### 3.4 Cost and Latency

Evaluates the operational efficiency of the system, particularly how quickly it responds to user queries.

Relevant criterion:
- Response Time.

## 4. Evaluation Approach

The proposed evaluation will use a curated set of academic questions based on the approved UTM academic documents.

The questions will cover different academic information categories, including:

1. Academic regulations and requirements.
2. Course registration procedures.
3. Programme handbook information.
4. Academic calendars and important dates.
5. Questions that cannot be answered using the available documents.
6. Questions outside the intended academic information domain.

The system's responses will be assessed against verified reference information. Response time, source relevance, answer correctness and scope-handling performance will be recorded.

## 5. Expected Evaluation Outcomes

UniGuide will be considered to meet its initial evaluation targets when it demonstrates:

- At least 80% correct answers for the curated academic question set.
- At least 95% citation-source correctness.
- At least 80% of responses meeting the defined clarity criteria.
- At least 95% correct refusal and redirection for out-of-scope queries.
- A 90th percentile response time of no more than 10 seconds.

These thresholds represent the proposed minimum evaluation targets for the initial version of UniGuide. Actual performance will be determined through testing.
